Why we started CraftKit

Hi I'm Katie, the founder. I want to take us back to the fond memories of playing and being transported to another world—one where all your friends are in on it.

The childhood moments that stick are rarely the ones on a screen.

So we build that magic. Costumes, a story to step into, games and creative activities. Kids run, laugh, create and work as a team, coming away with long lasting memories, not just another party or program.

Today we run parties in Hood River, Oregon and Melbourne, Australia. We hope to, one day, be able to take this magic all over the world!
