    Six weeks. One story. Real things learned.

    Each theme is a six-week story. Every session is one hour: ten minutes of lore and a fact or two, forty minutes of building, then ten minutes to test what they made and pack up.

    Week one kids build the world on a wall-sized backdrop. Later weeks add colour, wearable gear and working mechanisms, and the story grows with them.
