# CraftKit social ad copy

Edit the text after each `key:` and run `python3 build_comms.py` (from the `comms` folder). The social layouts in `../social/`
(HTML and PNG) are rebuilt from this file. Use ` // ` where you want a line break. Price tokens work here too: `{voucherOff}`, `{price}`.
Keep headlines short: they sit on a photo or a dark band at large sizes, and Meta covers the top 250px and bottom 340px of story frames.
Photo labels (the grey text on the placeholder) describe the photo to shoot or drop in.

## intro
text: Six overlay layouts in parent-friendly language: two 4:5 feed ads, a square voucher ad and a three-frame story carousel. Photo slots are placeholders; text sits on a dark scrim or band so it stays readable over any photo. On story frames the dashed outline shows the safe zone (top 250px and bottom 340px are covered by Meta's interface).
